CHROME HEARTS

OVERVIEW

Blender / Houdini / Nuke / Substance Painter - 2023

Chrome hearts is an American clothing and jewelry brand established in the late 1980s. They produce leather goods, home accessories, clothing and jewelry. Much of their solid sterling silver jewelry rose to prominence in the 2010s amongst street and youth culture. Their signature cross is the most recognizable symbol associated with the brand. This 3D render made in a combination of Blender & Houdini displays the brand's elegance and gothic style.
