CHROME HEARTS

OVERVIEW

Blender / Houdini / Nuke / Substance Painter - 2023

A modern homage to Rollei’s classic Rolleiflex TLRs. In collaboration with Kendall Dirks: we co-developed the storyboard; I modeled the entire exterior to spec using a 3D scan and calipers, then UV-unwrapped; Kendall handled scene assembly and animation. The goal was to showcase the brand’s elegance with contemporary presentation.
