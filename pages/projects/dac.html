SONY PHA2 DAC / 2025

Sony is well known in the audio engineering community for their high quality audio equipment. Many are unaware of the fact that Sony produceds amplifiers and DACs (Digital to Analog converter). The PHA2 DAC by sony is a slim pocketable amp that you can fit in your pocket. It delivers (specs here) for audiophiles on the go. his model was created in the summer of 2022 using Autodesk Maya. I textured the model in painter and rendered in cycles. I redid this project in 2025.
