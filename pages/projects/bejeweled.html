BEJEWELED

OVERVIEW

Blender / Photoshop / Nuke / PolyCam - 2024

A collaborative portrait with photographer Massimo Greco inspired by the idea of a queen adorned in cold, crystalline light. We wanted a jewelry-forward image with a surreal, icy mood. I assembled the 3D in Blender, adapting snake models from Sketchfab and building a 3D stand-in for the model to design and place the jewelry precisely. Compositing happened in Nuke and Photoshop, with final color and print/web tweaks in Lightroom. The project moved from concept to final image in about a week.
