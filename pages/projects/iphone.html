IPHONE 12 MINI

OVERVIEW

Blender / Photoshop - 2021 (Revised 2025)

The iPhone 12 mini was a slim, miniture version of the iPhone 12 release in 2020. This model was created as practice in 2021 to refine my modeling skills. The phone was modeled entirely front to back with subdivision surfaces, and used reference from my personal iPhone 12 mini and dimensions found online. Reusing the same model from 2021, the project was revised in 2025 with new lighting, and textures. All renders were done in Blender, with compositing done in Photoshop.
