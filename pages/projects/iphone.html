IPHONE 12 MINI

OVERVIEW

Blender / Photoshop - 2021 (Revised 2025)

One of my early product models—built in 2021 when I started taking 3D seriously, then refined in 2025. Modeled with clean subdivision in Blender and rendered in Cycles. I retextured and relit the scene in 2025 to meet my current standards for materials and reflections. The hand is a Photoshop composite from a licensed stock image to keep focus on the device while grounding scale.
