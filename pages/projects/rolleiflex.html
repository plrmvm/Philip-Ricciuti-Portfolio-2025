ROLLEIFLEX

OVERVIEW

Maya / Houdini / Nuke / Substance Designer - 2023 (Revised 2024)

Rollei has been making luxurious film cameras since 1920, appealing to high class consumers. They were popular during the early 1950s era of film cameras and are most known for their flagship Rolleiflex line of medium format TLRs. This piece showcases the elegance of Rollei as a brand and displays a modern advertisement for an era of cameras long gone. This piece was in collaboration with close friend and artist Kendall Dirks. Collaboration was done on the shot sequencing, storyboard and concept. I modeled each exterior piece of the Rolleiflex to spec with a 3D scan and a pair of calipers. Kendall was on deck all texturing, scene assembly, rendering, lighting and animation. In post, I was responsible for compositing shots in NukeX and assembling the final video in After Effects.
