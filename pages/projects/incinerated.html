INCINERATED

OVERVIEW

Blender / Gaea / CloudCompute / Photoshop / Houdini - 2024

A post-apocalyptic city concept image built around real-world geography from Rochester, NY. Eli and Eddie developed the concept and directed the shoot (with on-set help from Kendall Dirks and me). Eli handled retouching and masking. I used OpenStreetMap and public LiDAR to rebuild correct landforms for perspective match. The environment combined KitBash3D assets and scans of abandoned buildings; terrain was processed in Gaea. Assembly and rendering were done in Blender/Cycles for a grounded, cinematic result.
