MAHARISHI

OVERVIEW

Blender / Houdini / Substance Painter - 2023 (Revised 2025)

A product visualization inspired by Maharishi’s modern utilitarian aesthetic and its collaboration with Klean Kanteen. Modeled in Blender from my own bottle during a 2023 train ride—part study, part exercise. In 2025, I added new hero scenes that echo Maharishi’s military-meets-nature language: restrained color, functional materials, and rugged backdrops.
