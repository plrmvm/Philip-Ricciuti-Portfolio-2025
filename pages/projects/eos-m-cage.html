EOS M CAGE

OVERVIEW

Fusion360 / Blender / After Effects - 2025

A concept camera cage for the Canon EOS-M—designed to provide mounting points for grips, mics, monitors, batteries, and other accessories. Drafted to spec in Autodesk Fusion 360 using a 3D scan of the EOS-M to confirm clearances and attachment points. The goal was a part that could be machined or cast in metal. Final materials/lighting and hero renders were created in Blender/Cycles.
